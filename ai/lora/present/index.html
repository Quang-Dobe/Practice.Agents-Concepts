LoRA — Low-Rank Adaptation

Freeze every weight of a big pretrained model and train a tiny add-on correction per layer instead, usually well under 1% of the model's size.

~10,000×
fewer trainable parameters on GPT-3 175B
~3×
less GPU memory than full fine-tuning
350 GB → 35 MB
GPT-3 checkpoint vs adapter (r=4)
0.4%
of a 4096×4096 matrix at r=8
