What it is not

⚠

Common mix-ups

  • Not a retry. A retry tries again now; this refuses to try at all.
  • Not a health check. It reads real traffic, not a synthetic ping.
  • Not free. It adds tuning and monitoring you have to own.

When to reach for it — and when not to

✓ Reach for it when

  • You make synchronous calls that can stall your own threads.
  • A dependency degrades slowly instead of failing fast.
  • You have an acceptable fallback, or fast failure beats a long hang.

✕ Skip it when

  • The call is in-process — nothing to isolate.
  • The work is async and queue-based — the queue is the buffer.
  • The system is small enough that one slow dep can't cascade.
